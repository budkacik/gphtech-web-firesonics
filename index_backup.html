System

Three layers, one response time.

FireSonics fuses acoustic sensing with on-device inference so ignition events are flagged in minutes, not hours.

AI Detection

Neural models trained on thousands of ignition signatures separate flame from wind, traffic, and wildlife in real time.

Real-time Monitoring

A distributed sensor mesh listens continuously across forest terrain, streaming telemetry to the cloud every few seconds.

Early Warning System

Confirmed detections route straight to response teams with location, confidence score, and live audio confirmation.

About the project

Built for the moments before a wildfire becomes unstoppable.

FireSonics began as a research effort into acoustic signatures of combustion. Today it's a full detection stack — sensors, edge inference, and a cloud platform — designed to give response teams the one resource they never have enough of: time.
